Our Kutty Story

Life Is Just Like a Cup of Tea

Do you ever think about why tea tastes so good and feels like it gives us a spark while drinking it? The reason is that tea is connected to human life☕❤️. In our lives, we experience many things, such as obstacles, struggles, happiness, sadness, and so much more.When we prepare tea, we add different ingredients like ginger🫚, sugar🧁, milk🥛, tea powder🌿, and cardamom. If we taste these ingredients separately, each one has a different flavour. Some taste sour😣, some sweet🤤, and some bitter😨.But when we mix all these ingredients together and heat them over a flame🔥, they transform into something wonderful😉. The overall taste changes, and it gives us an energetic feeling. In the same way, our lives also change when we face different experiences. When the fire of struggles comes into our lives😤 and we overcome it to achieve success📈, we also become like tea – a beautiful combination of different experiences that makes us who we are.
